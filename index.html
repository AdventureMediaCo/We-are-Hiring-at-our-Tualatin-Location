We are Hiring at our Tualatin Location

JACK OF ALL TRADES, MASTER OF SUPPORT

We are looking for an energetic individual with a strong work ethic to join our growing Tualatin team. This person will be a team player who will serve in a critical support role covering all Four Wheel Camper installs and some install shop assistance; shipping and receiving; merchandising; cleaning; and organizing.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Four Wheel Camper installs: requires basic electrical install in engine bay and measuring / drilling holes in truck bed -- successful candidate will have some knowledge of working on vehicles and using basic tools
  • All shipping and receiving: requires ability to package outgoing shipments efficiently, choose best shipping rates, and use a computer to fulfill orders; requires safe handling of incoming shipments, receiving inventory accurately in computer system, and distributing to stock or customer parts shelf. Must be able to safely and efficiently operate a forklift to receive camper arrivals.
  • General store cleanliness including, but not limited to, garbage, recycling, vacuuming and mopping, bathrooms, kitchen, windows, floors, product and campers on display.
  • Merchandising and product facing.
  • This position provides the opportunity to grow into a full time install technician role provided the candidate is interested in such and demonstrates a strong work ethic, attention to detail, and quality workmanship in all aspects of the job
Find Us Here!

REQUIREMENTS

  • Ability to operate a forklift
  • Able to work professionally in a shop environment and proficiency with basic automotive tools
  • Attention to detail and ability to organize and prioritize particularly given the diversity of tasks required
  • Ability to lift 50+ lbs on a regular basis and be on your feet up to 8 hours per day
  • Ability to receive instruction, read directions and complete tasks without direct supervision
  • Proven integrity and ability to work on a team
  • Neat and professional appearance
  • Strong communication skills
  • Clean driving record (abstract will be required)

POSITION & BENEFITS

  • Tue-Fri 8 hours per day to start (32 hours per week) with option to become 40 as demand and responsibilities increase
  • $15 per hour DOE
  • Health insurance benefits
  • Reports to General Manager

Please send resume by email to: pdxsales@dasmule.com
